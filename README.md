# Sprouté

**Where elegance grows naturally**

## About Sprouté

Sprouté is a jewelry brand that celebrates the beauty of growth, transformation, and personal journeys. Our name comes from the French word "sprout" (*pousse*), symbolizing new beginnings and natural elegance. The pronunciation "Spring Route" reflects our philosophy of guiding each person along their unique path of renewal.

In Chinese philosophy, we embody 正春风 (*zhèng chūn fēng*) - "The True Spring Wind" - representing the gentle force that nurtures growth and brings authentic beauty to life.

## Our Philosophy

Every piece of jewelry we create is:

- **Handcrafted**: Made with care and attention to detail
- **Meaningful**: Designed to celebrate your personal journey
- **Unique**: Each piece carries its own character and story

We believe that jewelry is more than adornment - it's a reflection of who you are and who you're becoming. Like a sprout emerging from the earth, each design embodies the organic beauty of growth and transformation.
